- **High-leverage IR analytical blades (applied, not academic pedantry):** **Realism** (Thucydides Trap, Kautilya’s *Rajamandala/Shadgunya/Upayas*, Security Dilemma, Structural/Offensive vs. Defensive Realism), **Liberal Institutionalism & Complex Interdependence** (Keohane–Nye), **Constructivism** (norms & strategic identity), **World-Systems / Global IR** (Amitav Acharya’s Multiplex World), and **Classical Geopolitics** (Mackinder Heartland, Spykman Rimland, Mahan Sea Power)
- **Evolution of Indian Foreign Policy:** Nehruvian Non-Alignment (1947–62) → Realpolitik Balancing & 1971 Treaty (1962–91) → Look East, **Gujral Doctrine (1996)**, Pokhran-II & 2008 Nuclear Deal (1991–2014) → **Strategic Autonomy, Multi-Alignment & *Vishwamitra* Plurilateralism** (2014–Present)
- **India’s Nuclear Doctrine (Jan 2003 CCS):** Credible Minimum Deterrence (CMD), No First Use (NFU), massive punitive retaliation, CBW exception, civilian command via **NCA (Political + Executive Councils) & Strategic Forces Command** across the triad
- **Soft Power, HADR & 35M Global Diaspora:** Project Mausam, Buddhist diplomacy, Vaccine Maitri, HADR (*Dost, Karuna, Sadbhav*); 3 historical diaspora waves (Girmitiya, Western elites, 9M Gulf workers), **$125B+ remittances**, OCI rights vs. exclusions, Non-Combatant Evacuations (*Rahat, Ganga, Kaveri, Ajay, Indravati*), and diaspora transnational extremism friction (Canada / Five Eyes)

### Chapter 3: WMD Non-Proliferation, Strategic Export Control Cartels & Post-New START Arms Control Collapse
