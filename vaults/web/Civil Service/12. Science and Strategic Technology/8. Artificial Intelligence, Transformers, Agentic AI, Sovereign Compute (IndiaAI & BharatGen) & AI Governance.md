# Chapter 8 - Artificial Intelligence, Transformers, Agentic AI, Sovereign Compute (IndiaAI & BharatGen) & AI Governance

Artificial Intelligence has crossed from narrow pattern classification into general-purpose cognitive infrastructure capable of reasoning over code, orchestrating multi-step digital workflows, and designing novel biomolecules. For India, the strategic challenge is dual: breaking compute and linguistic dependencies on Western English-first foundation models through sovereign public infrastructure, while governing autonomous and synthetic-media risks without strangling startup velocity.

## AI and Machine Learning Taxonomy, Neural Physics and the Transformer Architecture

### Symbolic AI vs. Statistical Machine Learning and Capability Horizons

Classical **Symbolic AI** (Good Old-Fashioned AI, GOFAI) encoded explicit human rules, formal logic predicates, and decision trees into expert systems. While transparent and deterministic, symbolic architectures collapse when confronting noisy, high-dimensional real-world perception such as unconstrained speech, radiology scans, or vernacular idioms.

**Machine Learning (ML)** inverts this paradigm: rather than hand-coding rules, algorithms adjust millions or billions of numerical parameters via **mathematical optimization** (stochastic gradient descent minimizing a loss function) to infer statistical mappings directly from data. **Deep Learning** is a strict subset of ML employing multi-layered Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) that automatically construct hierarchical feature representations. ML training spans four core regimes:

- **Supervised Learning:** Learns input–output mappings from explicitly labelled pairs $(x, y)$ (e.g., classifying diabetic retinopathy from annotated retinal scans).
- **Unsupervised & Self-Supervised Learning:** Unsupervised methods discover latent structure (clustering, anomaly detection) without labels. **Self-Supervised Learning (SSL)**—the engine of modern foundation models—generates its own supervisory signal from raw unlabelled corpora by masking tokens or predicting the next token across trillions of web, code, and book sequences.
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL) & Alignment (RLHF / DPO / RLVR):** An agent maximizes cumulative scalar rewards through trial-and-error interaction with an environment (pioneered by 2024 Turing Award laureates Andrew Barto and Richard Sutton). Pre-trained Large Language Models (LLMs) are aligned to human intent using **RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)**—training a separate reward model on human preference rankings and optimizing the policy via PPO—or **DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)**, which eliminates the unstable reward model by optimizing directly over preference pairs via a closed-form classification loss. Frontier reasoning models further apply **RL with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)** against deterministic math proofs and unit-tested code.

Across capability horizons, every commercially deployed AI system today—including frontier LLMs, autonomous coding agents, and AlphaFold 3—remains **Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)** confined to bounded digital/statistical domains. **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)** denotes a still-hypothetical system matching human cross-domain transfer learning, physical common sense, and autonomous goal formulation, while **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** represents a speculative threshold surpassing collective human cognitive performance across all scientific and strategic tasks.

### Deep Learning and Statistical Physics - The 2024 Physics Nobel

On **October 8, 2024**, the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the **2024 Nobel Prize in Physics** jointly to **John J. Hopfield** (Princeton University) and **Geoffrey E. Hinton** (University of Toronto) *"for foundational discoveries and inventions that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks"*. The citation explicitly linked deep learning to statistical physics:

- **Hopfield Networks (1982):** Hopfield modelled associative memory as a recurrent network of binary nodes analogous to **magnetic spins in an Ising spin-glass**. Encoding memories sculpted a global energy landscape where stored patterns sat at local energy minima; presented with a corrupted input, the network relaxed downhill to reconstruct the original memory.
- **Boltzmann Machines & Backpropagation:** Building directly on Hopfield's energy formalism, Hinton introduced **Boltzmann Machines** governed by statistical mechanics' Boltzmann probability distribution ($P(s) \propto e^{-E(s)/k_B T}$), introducing latent "hidden" units able to learn probability distributions over complex data autonomously. Together with David Rumelhart and Ronald Williams, Hinton also popularized **backpropagation**—applying the chain rule of calculus to propagate output errors backward across deep layers—unlocking scalable multi-layer neural network training.

### Core Deep-Learning Architectures - CNNs, Diffusion and Transformer Self-Attention

Modern AI draws on three specialized neural architectures matched to data geometry:

- **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs):** Apply translation-equivariant learnable spatial filter kernels across pixel grids, capturing edges, textures, and organs hierarchically with far fewer parameters than dense networks—remaining staples in real-time computer vision, satellite imagery analysis, and radiology diagnostics.
- **Diffusion Models:** Learn to generate high-fidelity continuous data (images, video, audio, 3D molecular coordinates) by reversing a gradual Markov process that corrupts clean training samples with Gaussian noise. During inference, the model iteratively denoises pure random noise into coherent structures conditioned on text or molecular prompts.
- **Transformers and Multi-Head Self-Attention:** Introduced in 2017 (*"Attention Is All You Need"*), the **Transformer** discarded sequential Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs/LSTMs)—which bottlenecked parallel GPU training and forgot distant context—in favour of **Scaled Dot-Product Self-Attention**:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$

Every input token is projected into Query ($Q$), Key ($K$), and Value ($V$) vectors; computing pairwise dot products across the entire sequence lets every word attend directly to every other word in parallel (with $O(N^2)$ memory/compute scaling in sequence length $N$). **Multi-Head Self-Attention** runs multiple attention subspaces concurrently so distinct heads specialize in syntax, coreference, mathematical structure, or cross-lingual semantics.

Autoregressive decoder-only Transformers (GPT, Llama, Param2) generate output sequentially by estimating a probability distribution over the entire vocabulary for the **next token** and selecting from it—either via **greedy decoding** (picking the highest-probability token, the baseline textbook framing keyed as correct in UPSC Prelims 2026) or stochastic decoding (temperature/top-$p$ sampling). Because Western subword tokenizers are trained largely on English corpora—whereas **Indic languages comprise under 1% of Common Crawl web data despite India representing 18% of global population**—foreign tokenizers shatter Indic scripts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Sanskrit compounds) into $3\times$–$5\times$ more tokens per sentence, imposing a steep **"Indic token tax"** that inflates inference cost and shrinks effective context windows.

## Frontier AI Architectures (2024-2026) - Reasoning, MoE, Agentic AI and AlphaFold 3

### Structural Evolution Beyond Pre-Training Scaling

As easily scraped public internet text neared exhaustion by 2024–2025, frontier AI shifted from brute-force pre-training scaling toward six architectural breakthroughs that define the 2025–2026 landscape.

### Agentic AI - Architecture, Applications, Advantages and Risk Topology (2026 Mains GS3 Core)

Unlike passive generative chatbots that wait for human prompts and output static text, **Agentic AI** refers to goal-directed autonomous software systems powered by foundation models that can **perceive dynamic environments, decompose complex objectives into sub-tasks, invoke external tools and APIs, maintain persistent state memory, and iteratively self-correct** with minimal human supervision.

**Working Architecture (The Agentic Control Loop):**
An agentic system wraps a reasoning LLM inside four tightly coupled engineering subsystems:
1. **Planning & Reasoning Core:** Applies Chain-of-Thought or **ReAct (Reason + Act)** loops to break a high-level directive (e.g., *"reconcile district crop-loss claims against satellite SAR imagery and disburse relief"*) into an ordered dependency graph of verifiable steps.
2. **Memory Architecture:** Combines short-term working context (active scratchpad) with long-term **episodic and semantic vector memory**, enabling continuity across multi-day workflows and learning from past tool errors.
3. **Tool-Use & Interoperability Protocols:** Translates intent into structured function calls against external software—SQL databases, code sandboxes, web APIs, or payment rails—increasingly standardized through protocols such as the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** (explicitly adopted in India's **AIKosh MCP** rollout in 2026 to let agents query national datasets autonomously) and **Agent-to-Agent (A2A)** handoffs in multi-agent teams.
4. **Observation, Guardrails & Feedback Loop:** Inspects deterministic environment outputs (compiler errors, HTTP status codes, database constraints), evaluates progress against success criteria, and loops until completion or human-in-the-loop (HITL) escalation.

**High-Impact Applications:**
- **Software & Cyber Operations:** Autonomous software engineering agents write, test, patch, and deploy codebases; Security Operations Centre (SOC) agents triage zero-day alerts and isolate compromised endpoints in milliseconds.
- **Public Service Delivery & Multilingual DPI:** Voice-first vernacular agents combining Bhashini/BharatGen with DigiLocker, UMANG, and UPI can verify farmer eligibility, file PMFBY crop claims, schedule e-Sanjeevani tele-consultations, and track grievance redressal autonomously across all 22 Scheduled Languages.
- **Scientific Discovery & Clinical Workflows:** Autonomous "AI scientists" query literature, design molecular docking simulations via AlphaFold 3, and schedule robotic wet-lab synthesis; hospital workflow agents coordinate diagnostics, prior insurance authorizations, and ICU monitoring.
- **Supply Chain & Finance:** Real-time procurement agents dynamically re-route logistics during port disruptions and execute treasury compliance reconciliations.

**Advantages vs. Risks and Governance Challenges:**
Agentic systems multiply institutional productivity through continuous 24/7 execution, parallelized cross-system orchestration, and rapid adaptation to unstructured real-world exceptions. However, granting probabilistic models real-world **agency and write permissions** introduces acute systemic failure modes:

- **Compounding Error Propagation:** If each step in a 20-step workflow succeeds with $95\%$ reliability, end-to-end reliability collapses to $0.95^{20} \approx 36\%$—turning tiny hallucinations into irreversible downstream database or financial corruption.
- **Indirect Prompt Injection & Tool Hijacking:** Malicious instructions hidden inside an external webpage, PDF invoice, or email ingested by the agent can hijack its reasoning loop, exfiltrating private data or triggering unauthorized API transactions.
- **Accountability and Liability Voids:** When a multi-agent workflow erroneously denies a citizen's welfare ration or triggers a flash market crash, tort and administrative law struggle to allocate liability across the foundation-model developer, the agent orchestrator, and the deploying agency.
- **Privilege Escalation, Runaway Loops & Labour Displacement:** Over-scoped API credentials risk catastrophic cascading failures, infinite tool-calling loops drain compute budgets, and autonomous white-collar automation poses acute disruption to India's $280–300 billion IT services and BPO workforce.

### AlphaFold 2 and 3 - The 2024 Chemistry Nobel and Generative Structural Biology

On **October 9, 2024**, the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** was awarded half to **David Baker** (University of Washington) *"for computational protein design"*—creating entirely novel *de novo* proteins unknown to nature via *Rosetta* and *RFdiffusion* for vaccines, sensors, and enzymes—and half jointly to **Demis Hassabis and John Jumper** (Google DeepMind) *"for protein structure prediction"*.

For fifty years following Christian Anfinsen's 1972 Nobel postulate, predicting a protein's 3D folded structure solely from its 1D amino-acid sequence defied brute-force physics simulation. In 2020 (CASP14), **AlphaFold 2** cracked this grand challenge at near-experimental X-ray crystallography accuracy by coupling evolutionary **Multiple Sequence Alignments (MSAs)** with a specialized attention trunk (**Evoformer**) and 3D structure module—subsequently publishing an open database exceeding **200 million protein structures**.

In **May 2024**, DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs published **AlphaFold 3** in *Nature*, marking a deep architectural pivot:
- **Unified All-Biomolecule Scope:** Whereas AlphaFold 2 mapped amino-acid chains alone, **AlphaFold 3** jointly predicts atomic-resolution 3D complexes of **proteins, DNA, RNA, small-molecule drug ligands, metal ions, and post-translational modifications** (such as phosphorylation and glycosylation) within a single model—directly accelerating rational drug discovery and antibody design.
- **Pairformer + All-Atom Diffusion Architecture:** AlphaFold 3 replaces the heavy Evoformer with a streamlined **Pairformer** trunk (reducing reliance on MSAs) and swaps the rigid torsion-angle structure module for a **generative 3D diffusion module** predicting raw atomic coordinates directly.
- **Scientific Caveats:** Because AlphaFold 3 uses generative diffusion, it can occasionally **"hallucinate"** stereochemically plausible static structures inside intrinsically disordered protein regions (mitigated via cross-distillation from AlphaFold-Multimer). Furthermore, DeepMind initially withheld full open-source weights in favour of a non-commercial web server—sparking sharp global open-science debate before releasing academic inference code under restricted terms.

## The Compute, Memory and Energy Bottleneck

### Accelerators, Interconnects and the Memory Wall

AI progress is physically gated not just by algorithms, but by silicon geometry, memory bandwidth, and megawatt power envelopes:

- **GPUs vs. TPUs vs. NPUs:** **Graphics Processing Units (GPUs)**—led by NVIDIA's Hopper (H100/H200), Blackwell (B200/GB300), and **Vera Rubin NVL72** (entering full production in H2 2026 on TSMC 3 nm with 336 billion transistors per package), alongside AMD's Instinct MI300X/MI355X—dominate general AI training and inference via thousands of parallel Tensor Cores and the entrenched **CUDA** software moat. **Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)** are Google's custom systolic-array ASICs optimized specifically for dense matrix multiplications at lower power per FLOP. **Neural Processing Units (NPUs)** are ultra-efficient low-bit silicon engines embedded directly into smartphones and PCs for sub-10-watt local edge inference.
- **High Bandwidth Memory (HBM3e / HBM4) & NVLink Fabrics:** Autoregressive LLM and sparse MoE inference is overwhelmingly **memory-bandwidth bound**, not compute-bound: accelerators spend more time streaming multi-gigabyte weight tensors from memory than multiplying matrices. Co-packaging stacked 3D **HBM3e** (Blackwell: 192 GB at ~8 TB/s) and next-generation **HBM4** (Rubin: wider 2048-bit interface, 288 GB at ~19.2 TB/s per GPU) directly onto silicon interposers eases this bottleneck. Across multi-GPU clusters, proprietary scale-up fabrics—**NVLink 5** (1.8 TB/s per GPU) scaling to **NVLink 6** (3.0 TB/s per GPU across 72 liquid-cooled Rubin GPUs inside a single NVL72 rack)—fuse an entire cabinet into one unified memory domain.

### Gigawatt Data Centres - Electricity, Water Cooling and Nuclear Baseloads

The International Energy Agency's flagship *Energy and AI* (2025) and *Key Questions on Energy and AI* (2026) reports confirm a structural break in global electricity trajectories. Global data-centre electricity demand stood at **~415 TWh in 2024 (~1.5% of world electricity)**, surged 17% in a single year to **~485 TWh in 2025**, and is projected to **nearly double to ~945–950 TWh by 2030 (~3% of global electricity)** and reach ~1,300 TWh by 2035.

Physical engineering limits compound this energy surge:
- **Thermal & Water Stress:** Modern AI racks exceed **$120\text{ kW}$ per cabinet** (10–15$\times$ conventional enterprise server racks), rendering traditional chilled-air cooling physically inadequate. Hyperscalers are migrating to **direct-to-chip liquid cooling** and immersion tanks; yet evaporative cooling towers consume millions of litres of freshwater annually—intensifying local hydro-stress in water-scarce Indian data-centre hubs like Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Mumbai-Navi Mumbai.
- **24/7 Carbon-Free Baseload & SMR Linkages:** Training frontier clusters demands flat, zero-flicker baseload electricity alongside rapid load-swing buffering via grid-scale batteries. Because intermittent solar/wind alone cannot guarantee 99.999% uptime, global hyperscalers signed conditional offtake agreements covering **45 GW of Small Modular Reactor (SMR) nuclear capacity by 2026** (up from 25 GW in 2024)—creating a direct strategic convergence in India between the **IndiaAI data-centre expansion** (bolstered by Budget 2026–27's tax holiday till 2047 for foreign cloud workloads hosted in India) and the **SHANTI Act 2025 / 100 GW Nuclear Energy Mission**.

### Engineering Highlights of BharatGen and the Indic Model Wave

**BharatGen** solves two specific engineering bottlenecks that cripple off-the-shelf Western models in India. First, its flagship **`Param2-17B-A2.4B-Thinking`** (launched by PM Modi at the February 2026 Summit) is pretrained **from scratch** on ~22 trillion tokens with a custom Indic tokenizer covering all **22 Scheduled Languages**. Second, its hybrid MoE design combines 64 specialized experts (routing 6 per token) with **2 always-active shared experts** specifically preserving cross-lingual representations and fluid Indian **code-switching** (e.g., Hinglish or Tanglish inside a single sentence). Activating only $2.4\text{B}$ parameters per token lets `Param2` run full BF16 inference on a single $48\text{ GB}$ L40S or H100 GPU, making on-premise hosting viable for Indian banks (bound by RBI data localization), hospitals, and state ministries.

Complementing BharatGen's public-good weights, **Sarvam AI** leads enterprise vernacular voice and reasoning (shipping MoE **Sarvam-30B** and **Sarvam-105B** alongside *Saaras* telephony ASR and *Bulbul* TTS), **Soket Labs** released **EKA-1** (an open-source 26.3B sparse hybrid foundation model trained on ~7.5T tokens across all 22 Scheduled Languages + English, optimized for Indic math, coding, reasoning, agentic tool use, BFSI, and cybersecurity) on **November 3, 2026**, and **ZenteiQ** released open-source offline **Dhīr 2B** (2.03B dense) and **Dhīr 5B** (MoE) SLMs supporting English and **10 Indian languages** (not all 22 Scheduled Languages) on the same day. Outside the initial IndiaAI cohort, **Ola Krutrim** built **Krutrim-1 (7B)** and open-weight **Krutrim-2 (12B)** alongside INR-billed domestic Krutrim Cloud infrastructure, while **Bhashini** (MeitY's National Language Translation Mission) and **AI4Bharat** (IIT Madras) provide foundational vernacular speech/translation rails.

Hosted at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, the **India–AI Impact Summit 2026** marked the **first time the global AI summit series** (succeeding Bletchley Park 2023 on AI Safety, Seoul 2024, and Paris 2025 on AI Action) **convened in the Global South**. Drawing over 100 national delegations, 20+ Heads of State/Government, and ~6 lakh in-person attendees, the summit consciously reframed global AI discourse from Western existential-risk doom toward equitable developmental diffusion under the theme ***"Sarvajan Hitaya, Sarvajan Sukhaya"*** (*Welfare for All, Happiness for All*), structured around **Three Sutras** (*People, Planet, Progress*) and **Seven Chakras (Pillars of Action)**—including *Access for Social Empowerment*, *AI for Science*, and *Secure and Trusted AI*.

Key deliverables established India as a norm-shaping bridge between the Global North and Global South:
- **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact:** Endorsed by **92 countries and international organizations** (initial 88 on Feb 21 rising to 92 by March 2026) as a **voluntary, non-binding** multilateral declaration affirming national sovereignty, democratic access to foundational compute/datasets, and safe, resilient AI.
- **Global Public-Good Mechanisms:** Launch of the **voluntary, non-binding Charter for the Democratic Diffusion of AI**, the **Global AI Impact Commons** (cataloguing 80+ replicable public-interest use cases across 30+ countries), the **New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments** signed by 13 leading global and Indian model providers, and an **Equitable AI Transition Playbook** co-authored with the International Labour Organization (ILO).
- **Capital Mobilization:** Over **$250 billion** in announced investment commitments across India's AI compute, data-centre, and semiconductor value chain alongside MeitY's expansion beyond 38,000 GPUs.

## Global and Indian AI Governance - EU AI Act vs. India Techno-Legal DPI Approach

### The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) and the 2026 Digital Omnibus Shift

The **European Union AI Act**—the world's first binding, horizontal AI statute, which entered into force on **August 1, 2024**—exemplifies a rights-centric, precautionary regulatory regime exercising an extraterritorial "Brussels Effect" (with fines up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover). It classifies AI deployments across **four risk tiers** alongside a separate track for **General-Purpose AI (GPAI)** models (imposing heightened red-teaming and reporting duties on systemic-risk models trained above $10^{25}$ FLOPs):

- **Unacceptable Risk (Prohibited since February 2, 2025):** Bans social scoring, manipulative subliminal systems, untargeted facial-image scraping, emotion recognition in workplaces/schools, and real-time public remote biometric identification (barring narrow judicial counter-terror exceptions).
- **High Risk (Annex III & Annex I):** Biometrics, critical infrastructure, hiring, credit scoring, education, migration, law enforcement, and judicial administration—mandating pre-market conformity assessments, risk management, data-governance audits, logging, and human oversight.
- **Limited Risk (Transparency — Article 50):** Mandatory disclosure of chatbot interactions and machine-readable watermarking/labelling of deepfakes and synthetic content.
- **Minimal Risk:** Unregulated (spam filters, video-game AI).

**Critical 2026 Implementation Update:** While GPAI rules entered application on **August 2, 2025**, and EU/national enforcement for GPAI, prohibitions, and **Article 50 transparency obligations** went live on **August 2, 2026**, the EU's **2026 Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)** formally **postponed binding High-Risk AI compliance deadlines**—due to delayed harmonized technical standards—to **December 2, 2027 (Annex III high-risk systems)** and **August 2, 2028 (Annex I product-embedded systems)**, while introducing new prohibitions against non-consensual sexual deepfakes effective **December 2, 2026**.

### India's Techno-Legal "DPI-AI" Approach

Rejecting heavy pre-emptive omnibus legislation that could entrench foreign incumbents and crush domestic startups, India adopted an agile, **principle-based "techno-legal" governance framework** formalized in the **India AI Governance Guidelines (released November 5, 2025)** and consolidated at the February 2026 Summit. Built around **Seven Guiding Sutras** and a whole-of-government design, India's model pairs targeted statutory enforcement under existing laws—the **DPDP Act, 2023** (personal data consent and fiduciary accountability), the **IT Act, 2000**, competition law, and sectoral regulators (RBI, SEBI, TRAI, CDSCO)—with technical guardrails built directly into public digital rails.

Institutionally, the Guidelines established a three-tier governance architecture:
1. **AI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG):** High-level inter-ministerial body steering national AI policy and risk-innovation balance.
2. **Technology and Policy Expert Committee (TPEC):** Domain advisory body aligning technical standards with evolving model capabilities (including autonomous AI agents).
3. **IndiaAI Safety Institute (AISI):** Announced in January 2025 as an indigenous hub-and-spoke safety R&D body evaluating bias, explainability, privacy-preserving AI, and algorithmic auditing across **13 funded Responsible AI projects**—complemented by **CERT-In** advisories on adversarial AI (May 2023), generative AI security (March 2025), and the **CSPAI** AI-cybersecurity certification programme.

### Synthetic Media, Deepfakes and Provenance Guardrails

Modern diffusion models and zero-shot neural voice cloners have collapsed the marginal cost of fabricating photorealistic video and audio, threatening electoral integrity, biometric KYC verification, and financial authentication. From an engineering standpoint, purely reactive deepfake detectors are locked in a losing adversarial arms race against newer generators; durable mitigation therefore requires cryptographic content provenance standards (such as C2PA metadata credentials, imperceptible neural watermarking, and AISI-evaluated multimodal detection pipelines) embedded at model generation time.

To enforce platform-level compliance, labelling, and rapid takedown of synthetically generated information (SGI) across Indian cyberspace without waiting for standalone AI legislation, MeitY overhauled intermediary due-diligence obligations—detailed with full statutory precision in [[9. Information Warfare, IT Rules 2026 & Online Radicalization#IT Rules 2021 to 2026 Synthetic Media Overhaul|IT Rules 2026 (Deepfakes/SGI)]].

> [!warning] Prelims Traps & Atomic Distinctions
> - **LLM Mechanics, Greedy Decoding & Bias Trap (UPSC Prelims 2026 Direct Hit):** LLMs operate via next-token probability prediction and mathematical loss optimization—they **NEVER** produce inherently unbiased outputs, because they absorb and amplify training-corpus biases (including English-centric cultural and gender skews). Crucially, in UPSC Prelims 2026, Statement 1 (*"LLMs assign probabilities to the next possible words and then pick the one with the highest probability"*) was keyed as **CORRECT** (Official Key: **1 and 2 only**)—do not over-think or reject simplified greedy-decoding descriptions in generalist Prelims stems!
> - **AI Impact Summit 2026 Sutras & Non-Binding Trap (UPSC Prelims 2026 Q65 Direct Hit):** The New Delhi AI Impact Summit's Three Sutras are **People, Planet, and Progress**—NOT *"People, Planning, and Progress"*. Both the **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact** and the **Charter for the Democratic Diffusion of AI** are **voluntary and non-binding** (NOT legally binding frameworks), while the Declaration is organized around **Seven Chakras** (*Access for Social Empowerment*, *AI for Science*, *Secure and Trusted AI*, etc.).
> - **IndiaAI vs. BharatGen Ministry Ownership Trap:** **IndiaAI Mission** (₹10,372 cr) is implemented by **MeitY** (via Digital India Corporation). **BharatGen** was initially launched and seeded (₹235 cr) by the **Department of Science and Technology (DST)** under **NM-ICPS**, led by an **IIT Bombay** consortium (now a **Section-8 company**), and subsequently integrated/funded under MeitY's IndiaAI Mission. Do **not** conflate **IndiaAI** (MeitY AI ecosystem) with **ANRF** (DST statutory basic-research funder).
> - **BharatGen & Nov 2026 Indic Model Specs Trap:** **Param2** = 17B MoE text/reasoning LLM (2.4B active, **22 Scheduled Languages**); **Shrutam / Shrutam-2** = **Speech-to-Text (ASR)** (12 Indian languages); **Sooktam / Sooktam-2** = **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** (**11 Indian languages + Indian English** = 12 total); **Patram** = **7B Document-Vision VLM** (*DocBodh* framework). Do not swap *Shrutam* (hearing/listening $\to$ ASR) and *Sooktam* (speaking/hymn $\to$ TTS)! Among Nov 3, 2026 IndiaAI releases, **Soket `EKA-1`** (26.3B sparse hybrid) covers **all 22 Scheduled Languages + English**, whereas **ZenteiQ `Dhīr 2B` (dense) & `Dhīr 5B` (MoE)** cover **English + 10 Indian languages** (not all 22).
> - **2024 Nobel Attribution Trap:** **2024 Physics Nobel** went to **Hopfield & Hinton** (artificial neural networks / Hopfield networks / Boltzmann machines); **2024 Chemistry Nobel** went to **David Baker** (half, *computational protein design*) and **Demis Hassabis & John Jumper** (half jointly, *AlphaFold protein structure prediction*). Do **not** credit Baker for AlphaFold or Hassabis for *de novo* Rosetta design!
> - **AlphaFold 2 vs. AlphaFold 3 Trap:** **AlphaFold 2** predicted *protein structures* using the *Evoformer* trunk; **AlphaFold 3** (May 2024) predicts joint complexes of **proteins + DNA + RNA + small-molecule ligands + ions** using a **Pairformer + generative 3D Diffusion module**.
> - **MoE Inference Memory Trap:** Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) slashes active *compute FLOPs* per token (e.g., 2.4B active out of 17B in *Param2*), **NOT** total VRAM memory capacity—all 17B weights must still be loaded into memory, making MoE inference heavily **memory-bandwidth bound**.
> - **EU AI Act Timeline Trap (2026 Digital Omnibus):** While prohibited practices (Feb 2025), GPAI rules (Aug 2025), and Article 50 transparency enforcement (Aug 2026) are active, binding **High-Risk AI** compliance was **postponed under the 2026 Digital Omnibus** to **Dec 2027 (Annex III)** and **Aug 2028 (Annex I)**. Also, EU AI Act obligations are tiered by **use-case risk**, NOT by company nationality or revenue size.
> - **ANI vs. AGI Trap:** Frontier reasoning models and autonomous Agentic AI systems are still **Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)**; no nation or laboratory has achieved AGI.
