### Chapter 2: The Preamble, Union & Territory (Part I) & Citizenship Jurisprudence (Part II)
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Deep Dive *(High Prelims + Mains GS2 Yield)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Art 1–4 & Citizenship Acquisition/Loss Matrix` · `Prelims Traps` · `Mains Arsenal`

- **The Preamble:** sovereign philosophical matrix, 42nd CAA (1976) additions (*Socialist, Secular, Integrity* — **upheld in *Dr. Balram Singh v. UoI*, Nov 2024**), and legal status/amendability (*Berubari Union* 1960 → *Kesavananda* 1973 → *LIC of India* 1995)
- **Union & Territory (Part I, Art 1–4):** Art 1 ("Union of States" — indestructible union of destructible states), Art 2 (admission/establishment) vs. Art 3 (internal boundary/name change without mandatory state consent: *Babulal Parate* 1960), Art 4 (simple majority outside Art 368), vs. sovereign territory cession requiring an Art 368 CAA (*Berubari*, 9th CAA & 100th CAA 2015 Indo–Bangladesh LBA)
- **Citizenship (Part II, Art 5–11 & Citizenship Act 1955):** commencement criteria, single citizenship (Art 9), plenary parliamentary power (Art 11); acquisition (birth *jus soli* → *jus sanguinis* tightening, descent, registration, naturalization, incorporation) & termination/deprivation

- **Evolution of decentralization:** Art 40, CDP 1952, Balwant Rai Mehta (1957), Ashok Mehta (1977), G.V.K. Rao (1985), L.M. Singhvi (1986)
- **73rd CAA, 1992 (Part IX, Art 243–243O & 11th Schedule — 29 subjects):** compulsory vs. voluntary provisions, Gram Sabha (243A), 3-tier PRIs, SC/ST & 1/3rd (50% in 21+ states) women quotas (243D), OBC **"Triple Test"** (*Vikas Kishanrao Gawali* 2021), 5-yr tenure, age 21, disqualification validity (*Javed, Rajbala*), State Finance Commission (243I), State Election Commission (243K, *Kishan Singh Tomar* 2006)

- **Co-operative Societies (97th CAA, 2011):** Art 19(1)(c), 43B, Part IXB (243ZH–243ZT), and ***Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah* (2021)** striking down Part IXB for state cooperatives for lacking Art 368(2) ratification while upholding it for Multi-State Cooperatives

### Chapter 19: Constitutional Watchdogs, Statutory Rights Commissions & Comparative Constitutionalism
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Prelims Precision + Mains GS2 Comparative Authority
