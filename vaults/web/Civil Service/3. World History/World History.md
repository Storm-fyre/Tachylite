# 🧭 Subject Hub: World History `#subject-hub`

> [!abstract] Hub Design Principle
> **Zero Rote Trivia — Pure Causality, Ideologies & Global-to-Indian Linkages:** Built cleanly into 8 sweeping analytical arcs (zero Prelims over-slicing) engineered specifically to supply GS1 comparative rigor, GS2 IR historical depth, and high-altitude philosophical/historical examples for Mains Essay.

---

### Chapter 1: Enlightenment, Atlantic Revolutions, Napoleonic Order & the Vienna Backlash (1688–1815)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Causal & Ideational)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Atlantic Revolutions Comparison Table (American / French / Haitian)` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **Intellectual dawn:** Scientific Revolution & 1688 Glorious Revolution → European Enlightenment (Locke’s social contract, Montesquieu’s separation of powers, Voltaire, Rousseau’s *General Will*, Adam Smith’s anti-mercantilism) vs. Enlightened Absolutism & Divine Right collapse
- **Seven Years’ War (1756–63) & the American Constitutional Revolution (1775–89):** global theater entanglement (Plassey/Carnatic linked to Atlantic debts), mercantilist taxation crisis, Paine’s *Common Sense*, 1776 Declaration, Saratoga/Yorktown; **Global-Subcontinental Pivot** from First Atlantic Empire to Second Asian Territorial Empire (Cornwallis transferred from Yorktown to Bengal); 1787 US Constitution (federal separation, Bill of Rights vs. Three-Fifths Compromise slavery contradiction)
- **The French (1789–99) & Haitian (1791–1804) Revolutions:** Bourbon bankruptcy, Estates-General, Bastille, *Declaration of the Rights of Man*, Girondins vs. Jacobins & Robespierre’s Terror, *Levée en masse*, Thermidor; **Haiti (Saint-Domingue)** under Toussaint Louverture & Dessalines forging the world’s first independent Black anti-slavery republic (and France’s punitive 1825 indemnity)
- **Napoleon & the Congress of Vienna (1799–1815):** Napoleonic Code (1804), administrative centralization, Continental System (Anglo-French spillover to Egypt/Mysore), Peninsular ulcer, Russia 1812, Waterloo 1815; **Metternich’s Vienna Settlement** (Legitimacy, Compensation, Balance of Power, Concert of Europe)

---

### Chapter 2: Industrial Capitalism, Comparative Industrialization & Socialist Counter-Movements (1760–1914)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Economic & Ideological)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Comparative Industrialization Matrix (UK / Germany / US / Russia / Japan)` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **First (1760–1840) & Second (1870–1914) Industrial Revolutions:** why Britain first (Enclosures, coal/iron, navigable canals, Atlantic slavery & Bengal *Diwani* primitive accumulation, textile/steam breakthroughs) → Second Industrial leap (Bessemer steel, synthetic chemicals, electricity, petroleum, Fordism)
- **Comparative late-industrialization models:** Imperial Germany (*Zollverein*, universal banks, *Kartells*), United States (continental scale, tariffs, corporate trusts), Tsarist Russia (Witte’s state-led loans/Trans-Siberian), and **Meiji Japan** (state-incubated factories transferred to *Zaibatsu*)
- **Social rupture & welfare counter-moves:** urban slums, child/female labor, Luddism, Peterloo (1819), Chartism, Colonial Railway Paradox (exporting British industrial goods into India), and Bismarck’s 1880s preemptive State Socialism
- **Ideological counter-movements:** Utopian Socialism (Saint-Simon, Fourier, Owen), Anarchism/Syndicalism (Proudhon, Bakunin, Kropotkin), **Marx–Engels Scientific Socialism** (Historical Materialism, Surplus Value, *Communist Manifesto* 1848, *Das Kapital*), First International (1864), Paris Commune (1871), Second International (1889), and Bernstein’s Social Democracy

---

### Chapter 3: 19th-Century Nationalism, Unifications, the US Civil War & New Imperialism (1815–1914)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Geopolitical & Imperial Dynamics)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Italian vs. German Unification & New Imperialism Matrix` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **Metternich’s collapse & the Eastern Question:** 1830 & 1848 "Springtime of the Peoples", Ottoman decline, Crimean War (1853–56), and 1861 Russian Serf Emancipation
- **Nation-state unifications & the US Civil War:** **Italy (*Risorgimento*):** Mazzini’s idealism, Cavour’s Piedmont diplomacy, Garibaldi’s Red Shirts (1861–70); **Germany:** *Kleindeutschland*, Bismarck’s *Blood and Iron* (Danish, Austro-Prussian, Franco-Prussian 1870–71 wars, Alsace-Lorraine); **US Civil War (1861–77):** industrial free labor vs. chattel slavery, Lincoln, Emancipation, 13th–15th Amendments vs. Jim Crow — **and its subcontinental shock** (Union blockade causing Lancashire Cotton Famine & Deccan cotton boom/bust)
- **New Imperialism (Finance Capitalism — Hobson, Hilferding, Luxemburg, Lenin):** **Scramble for Africa** (1884–85 Berlin "Effective Occupation" cartography, Leopold’s Congo, Cape-to-Cairo vs. Fashoda, Ethiopia’s victory at **Adwa 1896**, Herero genocide, Boer Wars); **Qing China** (Opium Wars, Unequal Treaties, Taiping Rebellion, Spheres of Influence, Open Door, Boxers); Southeast Asia (Dutch *Cultuurstelsel*, British Malaya, French Indochina, US Philippines) & Latin America (Bolívar, Monroe Doctrine, Roosevelt Corollary); **Meiji Japan’s imperial pivot** (1868 Restoration, 1894–95 Sino-Japanese, 1904–05 Russo-Japanese War inspiring Asian nationalists, 1910 Korea annexation)

### Chapter 4: The Great War, Imperial Collapse, Paris Settlement & the Remaking of West Asia (1914–1948)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Boundary Redrawal & West Asian Roots)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `WWI Treaties & West Asian Contradictory Promises Table` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Contemporary West Asian diplomacy → `International Relations`

- **WWI structural triggers & industrial total war (1914–18):** Wilhelm II’s *Weltpolitik*, Triple Alliance vs. Triple Entente, Dreadnought race, Balkan powder keg, July Crisis & Schlieffen Plan; trench stalemates, U-boats vs. blockade, Brest-Litovsk (1918), US entry (1917), and **1.5 million Indian troops** across Ypres, Gallipoli, East Africa & Mesopotamia
- **Collapse of four continental empires & Paris Peace (1919–28):** Romanov, Hohenzollern, Habsburg, Ottoman fall; Wilson’s 14 Points vs. Clemenceau/Lloyd George; **Treaty of Versailles** (War Guilt, reparations, Polish Corridor, Rhineland, Mandates) & minor treaties (Saint-Germain, Trianon, Sèvres); structural defects of the **League of Nations**; 1920s diplomacy (Washington Naval, Ruhr hyperinflation, Dawes/Young debt loop, Locarno, Kellogg–Briand)
- **Dismantling the Ottoman Empire & Remaking West Asia (1914–48):** Young Turks, contradictory British wartime pledges (**Hussein–McMahon**, **Sykes–Picot 1916**, **Balfour Declaration 1917**); **Mustafa Kemal Atatürk’s** Turkish War of Independence → Treaty of Lausanne (1923) & secular *Six Arrows* (abolishing Caliphate 1924) alongside Reza Shah’s Iran; League Class-A Mandates (Syria/Lebanon, Iraq/Transjordan/Palestine); discovery of Gulf oil (1928 Red Line, ARAMCO, Seven Sisters); Palestine crisis (Peel 1937, 1939 White Paper, **UNGA Res 181**, May 1948 Israel, First Arab–Israeli War & *Nakba*)

### Chapter 5: The Russian & Chinese Revolutionary Arcs — Command Communism to Deng’s Pivot (1905–1989)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Comparative Communist Statecraft)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Soviet vs. Chinese Revolutionary Pathways Matrix` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **Tsarist collapse & Bolshevik Revolution (1905–28):** 1905 Revolution, Stolypin, WWI breakdown, Feb 1917 *Dual Power*, **Lenin’s April Theses & Oct 1917 Revolution**, Civil War (Red vs. White), War Communism → **NEP (1921–28)**, USSR (1922), and the Comintern’s **1920 Lenin–M.N. Roy Colonial Theses debate**
- **Stalinist command industrialization (1928–53):** *Socialism in One Country* vs. Trotsky’s *Permanent Revolution*; forced collectivization (*Kolkhoz*, *Holodomor* 1932–33), *Gosplan* Five-Year Plans, Great Purges (1936–38), and GULAG
- **Chinese Revolutionary Arc (1911–76):** Sun Yat-sen & 1911 Xinhai Revolution, Warlords, May Fourth (1919), CCP (1921) vs. KMT, 1927 Shanghai purge, **Mao’s peasant guerrilla shift**, Long March (1934–35), anti-Japanese United Front, 1945–49 Civil War & Oct 1949 PRC; Mao’s agrarian reform, Hundred Flowers, **Great Leap Forward (1958–62 famine)**, and **Cultural Revolution (1966–76)**
- **Deng Xiaoping’s Market Socialism Pivot (1978–89):** Four Modernizations, Household Responsibility System, TVEs, coastal **SEZs (Shenzhen)**, and the June 1989 Tiananmen crackdown pairing economic liberalization with CCP monopoly

### Chapter 6: The Great Depression, Totalitarianism, World War II & the Post-1945 Order (1919–1945)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Crisis, Ideology & Global Architecture)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Fascism vs. Nazism vs. Japanese Militarism Table` · `WWII Turning Points & Summits Matrix` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **The Great Depression (1929) & Keynesian shift:** Gold Exchange Standard fragility, Wall Street Crash, Smoot–Hawley tariffs, **colonial transmission** (50%+ Indian agricultural price crash & distress gold outflow to London); Keynes’s *General Theory* (1936) & **FDR’s New Deal** (Glass–Steagall, TVA, Social Security)
- **Rise of Totalitarianism & slide to war:** **Italian Fascism** (Mussolini, 1922 March on Rome, Corporate State, Lateran Treaty, Ethiopia 1935); **German Nazism** (Weimar collapse, Hitler, *Lebensraum*, Enabling Act, *Gleichschaltung*, Nuremberg Laws, *Kristallnacht*); **Japanese Militarism** (1931 Manchuria); Spanish Civil War (Guernica), failure of Appeasement (Anschluss, 1938 Munich), and **1939 Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact**
- **World War II, Holocaust & Total War (1939–45):** Blitzkrieg, Fall of France, Battle of Britain, Operation Barbarossa (1941), Pearl Harbor & Japanese SE Asia sweep; turning points (**Midway, El Alamein, Stalingrad, Kursk, Bagration, D-Day**); US Lend-Lease, Enigma, and **2.5-million Indian Army** (North Africa, Italy, CBI, Imphal–Kohima & wartime Sterling Balances); **The Holocaust** (Wannsee, Auschwitz) & Unit 731; Manhattan Project & Hiroshima–Nagasaki (Aug 1945)
- **Foundations of the Post-1945 Global Order:** Atlantic Charter, Tehran, **Yalta & Potsdam** (division of Germany/Europe), **1944 Bretton Woods**, **1945 San Francisco UN Charter**, and Nuremberg/Tokyo Trials

### Chapter 7: Decolonization in Asia & Africa, Anti-Apartheid, West Asian Flashpoints & the Third World Project (1945–1994)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis *(Global South & Post-Colonial Geopolitics)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Decolonization Trajectories Table (Constitutional vs. Armed)` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Contemporary NAM/Global South & West Asia → `International Relations`

- **Retreat of European empires:** post-WWII fiscal/Sterling exhaustion, Asian prestige collapse & superpower pressure
- **Asian & African decolonization:** Asia (India 1947, Indonesia Sukarno 1949, Malaya/Singapore 1957–65, Vietnam Ho Chi Minh & Dien Bien Phu 1954); Africa (Ghana Nkrumah 1957, Tanzania *Ujamaa*, Kenya Mau Mau, **Algeria FLN / Frantz Fanon / Évian 1962**, Congo Crisis & Lumumba, Portuguese wars triggering Lisbon’s 1974 Carnation Revolution)
- **South African Anti-Apartheid (1948–94):** Group Areas/Bantustans, ANC & 1955 Freedom Charter, Sharpeville (1960), Rivonia Trial (Mandela), Steve Biko/Soweto (1976), global sanctions, and 1994 transition & TRC
- **Third World Project & Neo-Colonialism:** **Dependency Theory** (Prebisch–Singer, Andre Gunder Frank, Samir Amin, Walter Rodney), Bandung (1955), NAM (Belgrade 1961), G77, UNCTAD (1964), and 1974 NIEO
- **The Post-1952 West Asian Crucible (1952–90):** Nasser’s Pan-Arabism & 1956 Suez Crisis; **1967 Six-Day War (UNSC 242)**, PLO, **1973 Yom Kippur War** & Camp David (1978–79); **1971 Nixon Shock & 1973 OPEC Oil Embargo** (stagflation, petrodollar Third World debt & the **Gulf–South Asia remittance corridor**); **1979 Iranian Revolution** (1953 Ajax legacy → Khomeini *Velayat-e Faqih*), Iran–Iraq War (1980–88), and the **Soviet–Afghan War (1979–89)** (Operation Cyclone birthing transnational jihadism & Af-Pak weaponization)

### Chapter 8: The Cold War, Neoliberal Turn, Soviet Collapse & Grand Ideological Synthesis (1945–2001)
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains & Essay Synthesis *(Grand Strategy + Political Philosophy)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Grand Ideological Synthesis Matrix (Capitalism / Socialism / Communism / Fascism / Neoliberalism / Indian Constitutional Democracy)` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Multipolar order & BRICS+ post-2001 → `International Relations`

- **Cold War bipolarity, European integration & proxy conflicts (1945–85):** Kennan’s Containment, Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, Berlin Blockade/Wall, NATO vs. Warsaw Pact; **European Integration** (Schuman 1950 → ECSC → 1957 Treaty of Rome → 1992 Maastricht EU); nuclear MAD & **1962 Cuban Missile Crisis**; proxy wars (**Korea, Vietnam, Latin America** — Cuba 1959, Chile 1973 Allende); **Sino–Soviet Split**, Nixon–Kissinger 1972 Shanghai opening, *Ostpolitik*, Détente (NPT/SALT/Helsinki) & 1979 collapse
- **The Neoliberal Counter-Revolution (1978–91):** 1970s stagflation, Chicago monetarism (Friedman, Hayek), Thatcher–Reagan deregulation, 1979–82 Volcker shock & Latin American Debt Crisis, and the **Washington Consensus** (IMF/WB SAPs)
- **Soviet Dissolution & Global Spillover (1985–91):** Brezhnev stagnation, oil crash, Chernobyl; **Gorbachev’s *Perestroika* & *Glasnost***, 1989 Eastern European Revolutions (Solidarity, Berlin Wall, Velvet Revolution, German Reunification), Aug 1991 coup & **Dec 1991 USSR dissolution** — collapsing India’s Rupee–Ruble trade bloc and accelerating 1991 liberalization
- **The Unipolar Moment (1990–2001):** Fukuyama vs. Huntington, First Gulf War, Yugoslavia/Srebrenica & Rwanda genocides (ICTY/ICTR & R2P), WTO (1995), 1997–98 Asian Financial Crisis, NATO expansion & Sept 11, 2001
- **Grand Ideological Masterclass (GS1 / GS2 / GS4 / Essay Core):** crisp comparative synthesis contrasting **Classical Capitalism, Welfare Capitalism, Neoliberalism, Democratic Socialism, Marxist Communism, and Fascism** against **India’s evolving Constitutional Political Economy**
