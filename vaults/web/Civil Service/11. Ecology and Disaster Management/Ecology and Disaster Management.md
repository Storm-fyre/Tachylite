### Chapter 1: Ecosystem Energetics, Biotic Interactions, Trophic Dynamics & Biogeochemical Cycles
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Conceptual + Top Prelims Science Precision
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Biotic Interactions & Biogeochemical Cycles Matrices` · `Prelims Traps`

- Open thermodynamic ecosystems: homeostasis, resistance vs. resilience, Holling’s adaptive cycle; Habitat vs. Ecological Niche (Hutchinson fundamental/realized & **Gause’s Competitive Exclusion**); Ecotone, ecocline & Edge Effect
- **Interspecific interactions & adaptations:** Mutualism (lichens, mycorrhizae, coral–zooxanthellae), Protocooperation, Commensalism, Amensalism/Allelopathy, Predation, Parasitism, Competition; halophytic vivipary/pneumatophores, xerophytes, Bergmann’s, Allen’s & Gloger’s rules
- **Trophic energetics & pyramids:** Grazing vs. Detritus chains, GPP vs. NPP ($NPP = GPP - R$), Lindeman’s 10% law; **Eltonian Pyramids** (Numbers upright/inverted tree, Biomass upright terrestrial/inverted marine, Energy always upright)
- **Succession & Biogeochemical cycles:** Primary vs. Secondary, autogenic/allogenic, xerarch vs. hydrarch; **Gaseous cycles** (Water, Carbon, **Nitrogen bacteria**: *Rhizobium/Azotobacter/Anabaena* fixation → ammonification → *Nitrosomonas/Nitrobacter* nitrification → *Pseudomonas* denitrification) vs. **Sedimentary cycles** (Phosphorus, Sulphur); NCAVES natural capital & **Rockström’s Nine Planetary Boundaries**
