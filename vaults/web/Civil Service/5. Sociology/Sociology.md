### Chapter 1: Indian Cultural Pluralism, Sociological Perspectives & Linguistic–Demographic Fabrics
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains & Essay Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Sociological Perspectives Matrix (Indological / Field / Marxist / Subaltern)` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **Foundational lenses on Indian society:** Indological/textual (*G.S. Ghurye, Louis Dumont*), Structural-Functional field-view (*M.N. Srinivas, S.C. Dube, André Béteille*), Marxist historical materialism (*D.P. Mukerji, A.R. Desai, Ramkrishna Mukherjee*), and Subaltern/Civilizational (*Ranajit Guha, B.R. Ambedkar, N.K. Bose, Surajit Sinha*)
- Colonial orientalism ("static village republics") vs. dynamic civilizational change; Redfield, Singer & McKim Marriott’s **Little & Great Traditions** (orthogenetic vs. heterogenetic change, universalization & parochialization)
- **Linguistic & genomic pluralism:** Indo-Aryan, Dravidian, Austroasiatic, Tibeto-Burman & Andamanese families; Eighth Schedule, three-language friction, **11 Classical Languages (Oct 2024 expansion)**; modern population-genomic refutation of colonial racial taxonomies (Risley, Guha)
- Religious pluralism, cross-cutting cleavages, and cultural dialectics of assimilation, acculturation, integration & composite syncretism (*Ganga–Jamuni Tehzeeb*)

### Chapter 2: The Caste System (Varna–Jati) — Theoretical Hierarchy, Mobility & Census 2027
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Deep Dive *(Core GS1 + GS2 + Essay)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Theories of Caste Comparison Table` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Art 15–16 affirmative action jurisprudence → `Polity`

- **Scriptural *Varna* vs. empirical *Jati* & core theories:** **Louis Dumont** (*Homo Hierarchicus* purity–pollution), Celestin Bouglé’s triad, **André Béteille** (*Caste, Class, and Power*: shift from cumulative to dispersed inequality), and **B.R. Ambedkar** (*Annihilation of Caste*: unnatural division of laborers & graded inequality); colonial Census codification (Risley 1901–31) hardening fluid boundaries
- **Post-Independence transformations:** **M.N. Srinivas’s** *Sanskritization, Westernization, Secularization & Dominant Caste*; **Rajni Kothari’s** politicization of caste; **Dipankar Gupta’s** shift from vertical hierarchy to horizontal competition ("discrete castes"); and Dalit Capitalism (DICCI)
- **Contemporary affirmative action & Census 2027 sociology:** Creamy Layer, Rohini Commission, **State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (Aug 2024 7-Judge SC/ST sub-classification)**, 2023 Bihar Caste Survey, and **Population Census 2027 Digital Caste Enumeration (Q.10; active rollout Nov 2026–Feb 2027)**
- **Persistence of structural boundaries:** matrimonial-app digital endogamy, *Khap* panchayats & honor violence (*Shakti Vahini* 2018), Dec 2025 SC matrilineal caste certificate ruling, and **Sukanya Shantha (Oct 2024)** abolishing caste-based labor and segregation in Indian prisons

### Chapter 3: Stratification within Religious Minorities & Subaltern Identity Discourses
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Minority Stratification Matrix` · `Mains Arsenal`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Art 25–30 & Art 44 UCC legal framework → `Polity`

- Refuting monolithic minority myths via **Imtiaz Ahmad’s** empirical framework of caste analogues among non-Hindus
- **Stratification among Indian Muslims & Pasmanda assertion:** *Ashraf* (claimed foreign noble lineages) vs. *Ajlaf* (clean occupational artisan converts) vs. *Arzal* (historically marginalized "untouchable" converts); the **Pasmanda Muslim movement (Ali Anwar)** demanding internal democratization and distributive justice
- **Caste hierarchies among non-Hindus:** Dalit Christians (church/burial segregation), Dalit Sikhs (*Mazhabis, Ramdasias* & *Ravidassia Dera* assertions vs. dominant Jat Sikhs), and Neo-Buddhists
- **Constitutional & empirical fault lines:** Paragraph 3 of the **Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950** & the **Justice K.G. Balakrishnan Commission**; Sachar (2006) & Ranganath Misra (2007) socio-economic deficits; Parsi demographic decline (*Jiyo Parsi*); sociological tension between Uniform Civil Codes and minority cultural autonomy

### Chapter 4: Family, Kinship & Marriage Systems — Functional Continuity vs. Structural Metamorphosis
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Iravati Karve's 4-Zone Kinship Table` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`

- **The Indian joint family beyond stereotypes:** coparcenary traditions (*Mitakshara* vs. *Dayabhaga*; daughters’ equal coparcenary right in *Vineeta Sharma* 2020); **A.M. Shah’s** distinction between commensal household and ritual/property family (refuting naive linear nuclearization) and **I.P. Desai’s** *functional jointness*
- **Iravati Karve’s four-zone comparative kinship map:** Northern (patrilineal, patrilocal, village exogamy, four-*gotra*, hypergamous asymmetry) vs. Southern (isogamous, cross-cousin/uncle–niece preference, territorial endogamy), Central, and Eastern zones; matrilineal transformations (Khasi, Garo, Kerala Nair *Marumakkathayam*); **Leela Dube’s** *Seed and Earth* gender-kinship metaphor
- **Marriage & contemporary household shifts:** caste endogamy, *Anuloma* vs. *Pratiloma*, persistent dowry inflation, Oct 2024 SC Child Marriage Prevention guidelines, DINKs, single-parent & "empty nest" households, "silver divorce", ART & Surrogacy Acts (2021) reshaping biological kinship, and non-heteronormative partnerships (*Supriyo* 2023)

### Chapter 5: Gender Regimes, Patriarchy, Care Economy, Women’s & Queer Mobilizations
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Deep Dive *(High GS1 + GS2 + Essay Yield)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `2024–2026 Gender Jurisprudence & Care Metrics Box` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** POSH, Mission Shakti & 106th CAA legal/delivery frameworks → `Governance` & `Polity`

- **Theories of subcontinental patriarchy:** Sylvia Walby’s private vs. public patriarchy, **Uma Chakravarti’s *Brahmanical Patriarchy*** (controlling female sexuality to police caste endogamy), and **Sharmila Rege’s *Dalit Feminist Standpoint*** intersectionality
- **Demographic & economic asymmetries:** Amartya Sen’s *Missing Women*, skewed child sex ratios & cross-regional bride trafficking; **Care Economy & NSO Time-Use Survey (2024)** unpaid domestic burden, **Claudia Goldin’s U-shaped FLFPR curve**, pink-collarization, STEM "leaky pipeline" & glass ceiling; political agency vs. *Sarpanch Pati* proxy syndrome
- **Gendered violence & 2024–2026 Supreme Court landmarks:** structural violence, technology-facilitated GBV & "Manosphere"/digital misogyny, healthcare workplace safety (National Task Force post-R.G. Kar), marital rape exception debates (BNS Sec 63), ***Dr. Jaya Thakur* (Jan 2026 — Right to Menstrual Health under Art 14/21/21A)**, and adoptive maternity parity
- **Women’s & Queer sociology:** waves of Indian women’s movements (19th-c. reform → nationalist → 1970s–80s autonomous → digital); *Hijra/Kinnar* social worlds, *NALSA* (2014), *Navtej Johar* (2018), and ***Jane Kaushik v. UoI* (Oct 2025 / `2026 (1) SCC 336` — horizontal public & private transgender employment equality + Justice Asha Menon Committee)**

### Chapter 6: Agrarian Social Structure, Rural Class Stratification & Peasant Mobilizations
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Agrarian Class & Mobility Framework Table` · `Mains Arsenal`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Land tenure, tenancy & farm economics → `Agrarian Systems`

- **Village transformation:** breakdown of William Wiser’s hereditary patron–client ***Jajmani* system** under monetization and market integration
- **Agrarian class taxonomy & dominant castes:** **Daniel Thorner’s** *Malik–Kisan–Mazdoor* triad; post-Green Revolution consolidation of **"Bullock Capitalists" (Rudolph & Rudolph)** and intermediate Dominant Peasant Castes (Jats, Marathas, Patidars, Kapus, Kammas, Reddys, Vokkaligas, Lingayats, Yadavs)
- **The agrarian stagnation & reservation paradox:** land fragmentation, groundwater/input distress, and dominant landed castes demanding backward-class (OBC/EWS) quotas amid blocked urban formal absorption
- **Rural precarity & Rurbanization:** **Jan Breman’s *footloose labour***, circular migration & neo-bondage; feminization of agricultural labor without land titles; Durkheimian anomic/fatalistic currents in farmer suicides; **Dipankar Gupta’s** *Whither the Indian Village?*, rural non-farm shift, and Rurbanization

### Chapter 7: Tribal Societies (Adivasis) — Continuum Debates, Displacement & Indigenous Assertions
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Tribal Policy Paradigms Matrix (Elwin / Ghurye / Nehru / Xaxa)` · `Mains Arsenal`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** FRA 2006, PESA, PM-JANMAN & LWE governance → `Governance`, `Polity` & `Security`

- **Geographical & institutional mosaic of 705+ STs:** Central Indian heartland (Bhils, Gonds, Santhals, Oraons, Mundas) vs. Northeast frontier polities (Nagas, Mizos, Khasis, Garos, Apatanis) vs. Island tribes; customary communal tenure, reciprocity, youth dormitories (*Ghotul, Dhumkuria, Morung*), animism/totemism; 75 PVTGs & DNTs (colonial Criminal Tribes Act stigma, Idate Commission)
- **Classical tribal-policy & sociological debates:** **Verrier Elwin’s** isolationist "National Park" vs. **G.S. Ghurye’s** assimilationist "Backward Hindus" vs. **Nehru’s** integrationist *Tribal Panchsheel*; **N.K. Bose’s** *Hindu Method of Tribal Absorption* and **Surajit Sinha’s** *Tribe–Caste / Tribe–Peasant Continuum*
- **Virginius Xaxa Committee (2014) & contemporary assertions:** internal class stratification/elite capture within tribes, Development-Induced Displacement (mining, mega-dams, fortress conservation), customary land alienation, *Sarna* religious code demands, and *Pathalgadi* self-governance assertions

### Chapter 8: Urban Sociology, Spatial Segregation, the Informal Proletariat & Digital-Age Alienation
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains & Essay Synthesis
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Classical Urban Sociology & Indian City Pathologies Table` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Municipal finance, Smart Cities & Tele-MANAS → `Governance`; Urban spatial models → `Geography`

- **Urban sociological lenses:** **Ferdinand Tönnies** (*Gemeinschaft* vs. *Gesellschaft*), **Georg Simmel** (blasé metropolitan psyche), **Louis Wirth** (*Urbanism as a Way of Life*), **David Harvey** (*Right to the City*), and Denis & Zérah’s **"Subaltern Urbanization"** in Census Towns
- **Spatial segregation & exclusion in Indian metropolises:** persistent caste residential clustering & religious ghettoization (rental/housing-society exclusion, culinary policing), gated enclaves, gentrification, slum economies, and **Amita Baviskar’s *Bourgeois Environmentalism*** (framing the urban poor as ecological polluters)
- **The urban informal proletariat:** circular construction migrants, feminized paid domestic workers, street vendors, waste-pickers, and algorithmically managed platform gig workers
- **Digital-age psychosocial crisis:** urban anomie, youth coaching-hub alienation (*Kota syndrome*), adolescent smartphone/social-media & online-gaming addiction (Economic Survey warnings & Online Gaming Act 2025 backdrop), and rising urban mental-health distress

### Chapter 9: Secularism, Communalism, Regionalism, Globalization & Inter-Generational Shift
> **Depth Rubric:** Analytical Mains & Essay Deep Dive
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Western Laïcité vs. Indian Principled Distance Matrix` · `Mains Arsenal` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Art 25–28 & 2026 9-Judge Sabarimala Reference → `Polity`

- **Sociology of Indian Secularism:** Western church–state wall & French *Laïcité* vs. *Sarva Dharma Sambhava* and **Rajeev Bhargava’s contextual *Principled Distance***; anti-modernist critiques (**T.N. Madan, Ashis Nandy**) vs. **Ambedkar’s Constitutional Morality** (intersecting the 2026 9-Judge Sabarimala Constitutional vs. Popular Morality hearings)
- **Communalism & Regionalism:** Bipan Chandra’s modern ideological anatomy of communalism, **Paul Brass’s *Institutionalized Riot Systems***, algorithmic hate-speech echo chambers & deepfakes; **Myron Weiner’s *Sons of the Soil*** nativism, private-sector domicile quotas, inter-state linguistic/riparian identity friction, and internal-colonialism grievances
- **Globalization & cultural metamorphosis:** **Yogendra Singh’s *Modernization of Indian Tradition***, **Leela Fernandes’s** post-1991 *New Middle Class* & conspicuous consumerism, **George Ritzer’s *McDonaldization*** vs. **Roland Robertson’s *Glocalization***, reverse cultural flows (diaspora, Hallyu, soft power), transnational care chains, and surveillance capitalism/data colonialism
- **Inter-generational & demographic shift:** geriatric isolation, feminization of aging, shrinking family support systems, and India’s emerging *Silver Economy*
