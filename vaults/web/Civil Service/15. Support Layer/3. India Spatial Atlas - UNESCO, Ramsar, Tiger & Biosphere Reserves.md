### C. Newest Tiger Reserves & Largest Conservation Reserve (2023–2025)

- **58th TR — Madhav TR (MP, Mar 2025):** Lies **wholly within Shivpuri district, MP** *(**Prelims 2026 PYQ Trap:** NOT shared with Rajasthan!)*; contains **Sakhya Sagar Ramsar Site**; links to Kuno–Ranthambore dry deciduous corridor.
- **57th TR — Ratapani TR (MP, Dec 2024):** Raisen & Sehore districts; surrounds **Bhimbetka UNESCO Rock Shelters**; **Betwa** catchment.
- **56th TR — Guru Ghasidas–Tamor Pingla TR (Chhattisgarh, Nov 2024):** **2,829.39 sq km** (**3rd largest TR** after Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam & Manas); links Sanjay-Dubri (MP), Bandhavgarh (MP), and Palamau (Jharkhand).
- **54th & 55th TRs (2023):** **Veerangana Durgavati TR** (MP — merged Nauradehi WLS, MP's largest TR; became **3rd Cheetah habitat** & **India's 1st Tiger Reserve to host African cheetahs** on Oct 7, 2026) & **Dholpur–Karauli TR** (Rajasthan — Chambal basin).
- **Tsarap Chu Conservation Reserve (Himachal Pradesh, May 7, 2025):** **1,585 sq km** in Spiti Valley — **India's LARGEST Conservation Reserve**; snow leopard corridor linking **Kibber WLS** & **Chandratal WLS** at confluence of Unam River & Charap Nala.

## High-Frequency Recall Drills (5 Exam Trap Patterns)

1. **The "Triple-Crown vs. Missing-Tag" Conservation Trap *(Manas, Khangchendzonga, Cold Desert)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Treating all 18 Biosphere Reserves as UNESCO MAB sites, or assuming **Manas** (a UNESCO Natural WHS + Tiger Reserve + BR) is also inside UNESCO's MAB network.
   - **Kill Switch:** Only **13 of 18 BRs** are in UNESCO MAB WNBR (**Cold Desert BR, HP** became **13th on Sept 27, 2025**). The **5 Non-MAB BRs** are **Great Rann of Kutch, Dibru–Saikhowa, Manas, Dihang–Dibang, and Seshachalam**. **Khangchendzonga** is India's **ONLY Mixed WHS** (and holds the Triple Crown: NP + Mixed WHS + MAB).

2. **The "State-Exclusion & Serial-Component" Trap *(Western Ghats, Maratha Forts, Mountain Railways, Zero-TR States)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Claiming all 12 *Maratha Military Landscapes* forts lie in Maharashtra, placing Gujarat/Goa inside the UNESCO Western Ghats serial list, including Matheran under Mountain Railways, or putting a Tiger Reserve in Gujarat/Goa/Himachal.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Maratha Forts (44th WHS, 2025)** = 11 Maharashtra + **1 Tamil Nadu (Gingee)**; **Western Ghats WHS** = **4 States ONLY** (MH, KA, KL, TN); **Mountain Railways** = Darjeeling, Nilgiri, Kalka–Shimla only; **Gujarat, Goa, HP, Punjab, Haryana, Sikkim, Meghalaya, Manipur, Nagaland, Tripura = ZERO Tiger Reserves!**

3. **The "Ramsar Name-State Mirage & Legal Exclusions" Trap *(Patna BS, Udaipur Jheel, Montreux Record, 2017 Rules)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Placing *Patna Bird Sanctuary* in Bihar, *Udaipur Jheel* in Rajasthan, listing *Chilika* as still on the Montreux Record, or claiming river channels and paddy fields qualify under India's Wetlands Rules 2017.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Patna BS** = **Etah, UP**; **Udaipur Jheel** = **West Champaran, Bihar**; **Montreux Record** = ONLY **Keoladeo (RJ) & Loktak (MN)** *(Chilika exited in 2002)*; **Wetlands Rules 2017** explicitly **exclude river channels, paddy fields, and man-made salt/aquaculture tanks**; **2025–26 "State Firsts"** = Udhwa (Jharkhand), Khecheopalri (Sikkim), Kopra (Chhattisgarh), Glaw Lake (Arunachal), Sukhna (Chandigarh).

4. **The "River-to-Park Swap" Trap *(Corbett, Silent Valley, Panna, Beas CR)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Running the Ganga through Jim Corbett, the Kaveri through Silent Valley, or claiming Indus River Dolphins inhabit Ladakh's Indus River inside India.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Jim Corbett** = **Ramganga** (Ganga flows through **Rajaji**); **Silent Valley** = **Kunthipuzha** (west-flowing Bharathapuzha tributary); **Panna** = **Ken River**; **India's ONLY 3 Indus River Dolphins** survive strictly in Punjab's **Beas Conservation Reserve** above Harike Barrage!

5. **The "2025–2026 Numeric & Habitat Expansion" Trap *(Cheetah, Lion, National Parks 106 vs 107)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Stating Kuno is still India's sole cheetah habitat, claiming Asiatic Lions live strictly inside protected Gir forests, or stumbling over 106 vs 107 National Parks.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Project Cheetah** now spans **3 MP sites** (**Kuno, Gandhi Sagar, Veerangana Durgavati TR** — ~52–53 cheetahs incl. Feb 2026 **Botswana** batch; **Veerangana Durgavati** is the **1st notified Tiger Reserve** with co-existing resident tigers); **Asiatic Lions = 891 (2025)** with **>50% outside protected areas** & **Barda WLS** as 2nd home; **National Parks = 106** (official WII/PIB Mar 2026 list — becomes **107** only if Assam's March 2025 *Sikhna Jwhwlao NP* state notification is added).
