## High-Frequency Recall Drills (5 Exam Trap Patterns)

1. **The "Obiter vs. Binding Ratio" Constitutional Trap *(Davinder Singh, AMU, Article 370)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Claiming the 7-judge bench in *Davinder Singh* (2024) mandated creamy-layer exclusion for SCs/STs, claiming *AMU* (2024) declared AMU a minority institution, or claiming *In Re: Art 370* (2023) upheld downgrading States into UTs under Art 3.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Davinder Singh** binding ratio = States MAY sub-classify SCs/STs (*creamy layer was non-binding concurring obiter by 4 judges*); **AMU** overruled *Azeez Basha* on statutory incorporation and sent AMU's factual status to a 3-judge bench; **Art 370** upheld CO 273, **struck down CO 272's Art 367 route**, and **expressly left Art 3 State-to-UT downgrading undecided**.

2. **The "Overruled Landmark & Lone Dissenter" Match Trap *(MADA, Lalta Prasad Vaish, Property Owners, Sita Soren)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Treating mineral **royalty as a tax** (outdated *India Cement* rule), assigning **industrial alcohol** exclusively to the Union (outdated *Synthetics & Chemicals* rule), or citing *P.V. Narasimha Rao* (1998) immunity for legislative bribery.
   - **Kill Switch:** **MADA (8:1)** = Royalty is **NOT a tax**, States tax mineral rights/land (arrears from Apr 1, 2005 in 12 instalments); **Lalta Prasad Vaish (8:1)** = States regulate **industrial alcohol** under Entry 8 List II; **Property Owners (8:1)** = **NOT all private property** is a "material resource" under Art 39(b); **Sita Soren (7:0)** = **ZERO bribery immunity** under Arts 105(2)/194(2).

3. **The "2025–2026 Reversal & Pending Verdict" Chronology Trap *(16th Presidential Reference, Vanashakti Trilogy, Sabarimala 9-Judge)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Citing April 2025's judicially fixed Governor timelines/"deemed assent" (*TN Governor*) as settled law, citing May 2025's total ban on *ex-post facto* ECs (*Vanashakti I*) as final, or fabricating a final holding for the 9-judge *Sabarimala* reference.
   - **Kill Switch:** **16th Presidential Reference (Nov 20, 2025, 5:0)** rejected fixed timelines & deemed assent (yet barred permanent pocket vetoes and permitted limited mandamus for prolonged inaction); **Vanashakti III (July 29, 2026, 3-judge)** upheld the **2017 Notification** (time-bound delegated legislation) while **quashing the 2021 OM prospectively**; **Sabarimala 9-Judge** was **RESERVED on May 14, 2026** and remains pending as of Nov 2026.

4. **The "MSP Formula & RBI Buffer Band" Numeric Trap *(Swaminathan vs GoI; Jalan 2019 vs May 2025 Review)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Equating Government of India's MSP pricing formula with Swaminathan's **$C_2 + 50\%$**, or citing the outdated 5.5%–6.5% RBI CRB ceiling after May 2025.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Swaminathan (NCF)** = **$C_2 + 50\%$** (includes imputed land rent & capital interest) vs **GoI MSP** = **$A_2 + FL + 50\%$**; **Bimal Jalan ECF (2019)** set CRB at **5.5%–6.5%**, whereas RBI's **May 2025 5-year review** widened CRB to **5.50%–7.50%** (kept at 7.50% for FY25; ₹2.68 lakh cr surplus transfer).

5. **The "15th vs. 16th Finance Commission Horizontal Weight Swap" Trap *(Feb 2026 Panagariya Report)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Retaining 15th FC weights (Income Distance 45%, Area 15%, Tax & Fiscal Effort 2.5%) for the 2026–31 award period, or claiming the 16th FC raised vertical devolution to 42%/50%.
   - **Kill Switch:** **16th FC (2026–31)** kept vertical devolution at **41%**; **DROPPED Tax & Fiscal Effort (2.5%)**; **INTRODUCED Contribution to GDP (10%)**; trimmed Income Distance (**45% $\rightarrow$ 42.5%**), Area (**15% $\rightarrow$ 10%**), and Demographic Performance (**12.5% $\rightarrow$ 10%**); raised Population 2011 (**15% $\rightarrow$ 17.5%**); and held Forest & Ecology steady at **10%**.
