> [!warning] Prelims Traps & Atomic Distinctions
> - **World Bank ICSID Exclusion:** India is a founding member of IBRD, IDA, IFC, and MIGA, yet has **NEVER joined ICSID** (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes), citing investor-biased arbitration rules.
> - **ICJ vs. ICC:** Both sit in **The Hague**, yet **ICJ** is a UN Principal Organ adjudicating **state-vs-state** disputes (India = Party), while **ICC** is an independent treaty court prosecuting **individuals** (India = **NOT a Party**).
> - **UN Specialized Agency Trap:** **WTO, IAEA, OPCW, and Ramsar** are **NOT UN Specialized Agencies** (even though WTO/IAEA/OPCW cooperate with the UN). **UNEP and UNDP** are UN Programmes (UNGA), NOT Specialized Agencies.
> - **SDR Basket Trap:** The IMF's SDR currency basket contains **ONLY 5 currencies** (USD, Euro, Chinese Renminbi/Yuan [added 2016], Japanese Yen, British Pound)—the **Indian Rupee is NOT included**.

## High-Frequency Recall Drills (5 Exam Trap Patterns)

1. **The "2024–2026 Expanded Bloc Membership" Trap *(BRICS+, ASEAN, SCO, CSC, G21)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Using outdated counts (ASEAN = 10, SCO = 9, BRICS = 9, CSC = 4), placing **Saudi Arabia** as a completed BRICS full member, or putting **Turkmenistan** inside SCO.
   - **Kill Switch:** **ASEAN = 11** (**Timor-Leste** acceded **Oct 26, 2025**); **SCO = 10** (**Belarus** joined **July 4, 2024**; Turkmenistan stays out); **BRICS+ = 10 full members** (**Indonesia** became 10th on **Jan 6, 2025**; **India chairs 2026**; Saudi Arabia invited/uncompleted, Argentina declined); **CSC = 5 full members** (**Bangladesh** joined **Aug 30, 2024**, Secretariat **Colombo**); **G21** includes **African Union** (**USA chairs 2026**).

2. **The "India's Strategic Non-Membership" Sniper List *(NSG, ICSID, ICC, NPT/CTBT/TPNW, ASEAN, BBNJ Ratification)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Claiming India belongs to all 4 WMD export regimes, all 5 World Bank arms, or both Hague courts (ICJ & ICC), or claiming India ratified BBNJ.
   - **Kill Switch:** India is in **3 of 4 export cartels** (**MTCR, Wassenaar, Australia Group** — **NOT NSG**, where China blocks despite India's 2008 Waiver); **4 of 5 World Bank arms** (**NOT ICSID**); **ICJ Party, NOT ICC**; **NON-SIGNATORY** to **NPT, CTBT, TPNW**; **SIGNED but NOT YET RATIFIED BBNJ** (entered into force **Jan 17, 2026**).

3. **The "Publisher-Inversion Index" Trap *(GHI, CPI, Press Freedom, WIR/TDR, LPI, EPI)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Matching Global Hunger Index to FAO, Press Freedom to UNESCO, Corruption Perceptions to World Bank, World Investment Report to WTO, or EPI to UNEP.
   - **Kill Switch:** **GHI** = Concern Worldwide + Welthungerhilfe; **CPI** = Transparency International; **Press Freedom** = RSF; **WIR & TDR** = UNCTAD; **LPI & B-READY** = World Bank; **WEO & GFSR** = IMF; **EPI** = Yale/Columbia; **CCPI** = Germanwatch/NewClimate/CAN.

4. **The "Triple-Chemical & Ozone/Marine Treaty" Swap Trap *(Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm, Minamata, Kigali, ISA)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Swapping **Rotterdam (PIC)** with **Basel (hazardous/plastic/e-waste)**, including radioactive waste inside Basel, claiming HFCs deplete ozone under Kigali, or placing **ISA HQ** in Geneva/New York.
   - **Kill Switch:** **Basel** = transboundary waste (*excludes radioactive!*); **Rotterdam** = Prior Informed Consent (PIC); **Stockholm** = POPs (**COP-12 May 2025** added **Chlorpyrifos, LC-PFCAs, MCCPs**); **Minamata** = Mercury; **Kigali** = phases down **HFCs** (*climate GHGs, zero ODP!*); **ISA HQ = Kingston, Jamaica** (under UNCLOS — US not a party!).

5. **The "Regional Outlier & HQ Swap" Trap *(IORA, BIMSTEC, NDB, AIIB, UNEP, Ramsar)*:**
   - **UPSC Bait:** Placing Maldives/Pakistan inside BIMSTEC, Pakistan/Myanmar inside IORA, USA/Japan inside AIIB, naming Beijing as NDB HQ, or calling Ramsar a UN convention.
   - **Kill Switch:** **BIMSTEC (Dhaka)** = 7 members (**NO Maldives/Pakistan**); **IORA (Mauritius; India Chair 2025–27)** = 23 members (**NO Pakistan/Myanmar**, **YES France!**); **NDB = Shanghai** *(equal founder voting, 9 members incl. **Algeria May 2025**)* vs **AIIB = Beijing** *(India 2nd shareholder, NO US/Japan)*; **UNEP = Nairobi** *(only UN HQ in Global South)*; **Ramsar (Gland)** = **NOT a UN treaty**!
