### Chapter 2: The Developmental State, Five-Year Plans & the 1991 Structural Adjustment Watershed
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Economic History *(PRIMARY ANCHOR — 360° for 1947–1991 Macro Trajectory)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Five-Year Plans & 1991 LPG Reforms Matrix` · `Prelims Traps` · `Whole-UPSC Spillover`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** Political/coalition arc of 1947–91 → `Modern Indian History`; Agrarian land reforms/Green Rev → `Agrarian Systems`

- **Planning lineage:** Visvesvaraya (1934), NPC (1938), Bombay Plan (1944), People’s/Gandhian/Sarvodaya Plans, Planning Commission (1950) & NDC (1952)
- **Plan trajectory (1st–12th Plans):** Harrod–Domar 1st Plan → **Mahalanobis heavy-industry ISI 2nd Plan & IPR 1956 (Schedules A/B/C)** → Gadgil–Mukherjee formula, Plan Holidays, Rolling Plans, Hindu Rate of Growth; **License–Permit–Quota Raj** (IDRA 1951, MRTP 1969, FERA 1973, 1969/80 bank nationalizations)
- **The 1991 BoP Crisis & Structural Adjustment:** 1980s fiscal/twin deficits, short-term external debt, USSR collapse & Gulf War oil shock → IMF conditionalities, Washington Consensus, Rupee devaluation, July 1991 Industrial Policy, Chelliah tax & Narasimham banking reforms → NITI Aayog (2015) & market-led regulatory state

### Chapter 5: Fiscal Architecture, Income-Tax Act 2025, Next-Gen GST 2.0 & Public Debt Sustainability
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Deep Dive *(Top 2026–2027 Prelims + Mains GS3 Yield)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Budget Deficits, Income-Tax 2025 & GST 2.0 Comparison Matrix` · `Prelims Traps` · `Mains Arsenal`
> **Provisional Context Bridge:** 16th FC horizontal devolution & GST Council *Mohit Minerals* → `Polity`

- **Budgetary mechanics & deficits:** Revenue vs. Capital Account, Capex "crowding-in" multipliers; Revenue, Effective Revenue, Fiscal, Primary & Monetized Deficits; Zero-Based, Outcome & Gender Budgeting; WMA, Sovereign Green Bonds, DIPAM, NMP 2.0 & Budget 2026–27 Dedicated CPSE REITs
- **Direct taxes & *Income-Tax Act, 2025* (Effective April 1, 2026):** replaces 1961 Act — unified **"Tax Year"** (eliminating Previous vs. Assessment Year), streamlined schedules, New vs. Old regime, **MAT cut 15% → 14% (Budget 2026–27)**, OECD 15% GMT, Angel Tax abolition, Capital Gains, STT, Equalization Levy, VDA taxation, FAST-DS 2026; Tax Buoyancy, Elasticity & Laffer Curve
- **Indirect taxes & *Next-Gen GST 2.0* (56th GST Council, Effective Sept 22, 2025):** Customs/inverted duty; destination-based GST, ITC chain, e-Way/e-Invoicing, GSTAT, and **GST 2.0 simplification** collapsing 4 slabs into **2 core slabs (5% Merit & 18% Standard) + 40% De-merit/Sin slab** (abolishing 12% & 28%)
- **Public Debt & FRBM Evolution:** FRBM Act 2003, N.K. Singh escape clauses, off-budget borrowings & **Post-FY26 shift to Central Government Debt-to-GDP Anchor (targeting 50 ± 1% by March 2031)**

### Chapter 12: Labor Economics, Employment Metrics, the Enforced Four Labour Codes & the Demographic Window
> **Depth Rubric:** Dual-Core Deep Dive *(Top 2026–2027 Prelims + Mains GS3 Yield)*
> **Gated Obsidian Elements:** `Enforced Four Labour Codes Comparison Matrix` · `Employment Metrics Table (PLFS / ASUSE / EPFO)` · `Prelims Traps` · `Mains Arsenal`

- **Labor measurement & structural informality:** **PLFS** (Usual Status `ps+ss` vs. CWS, LFPR, WPR, UR), ASUSE, ASI, EPFO, e-Shram; ~85–90% informality, disguised farm unemployment, stalled Lewis-model shift, "jobless growth" & **Generative AI white-collar displacement**; **Employment-Linked Incentive (ELI)** schemes
- **FLFPR & Care Economy:** Claudia Goldin’s U-curve, rural unpaid family helpers vs. low urban formal absorption, Time-Use Survey care burden & creche/safety gaps
- **The Four Labour Codes — Officially Enforced November 21, 2025 (Repealing 29 Central Acts):**
  - *Code on Wages (2019):* universal minimum wages & statutory National Floor Wage
  - *Industrial Relations Code (2020):* retrenchment prior-permission threshold raised 100→300 workers, fixed-term employment parity, negotiating union norms
  - *Code on Social Security (2020):* statutory recognition & welfare funds for **Gig, Platform & Unorganized Workers**, ESIC/EPFO expansion
  - *OSHWC Code (2020):* inter-state migrant portability, unified licensing & women’s night-shift safety
- **Demographic Dividend closing window:** North–South fertility split, ~2040–50 dependency window & the risk of "growing old before growing rich"
